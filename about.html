About This Site

This site began as just an assignment for my Web Programming class. I thought about something I loved and I made it about my 3 huskies. But then I wanted some inspiration and decided to check out GitHub for website portfolios. I found a really cool project that resembled Apple's Notes app.

This project really hit home. I am a Computer Science student and I work at Apple Lenox Square. I want a cooler way to show my resume. Something that really captures who I am and what I like.

My goal is to rebuild it by hand, not just copy it. In doing this I hope to customize it to represent me more and learn more about web/app creations!
